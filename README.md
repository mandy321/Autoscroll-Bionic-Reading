# Comfortable Reading Auto-Scroll (Premium Companion)

A premium, performance-optimized reading companion designed to eliminate visual fatigue, increase reading speed, and assist neurodivergent readers (ADHD, Dyslexia) in maintaining deep focus across the web.

---

This companion runs as a userscript. You can install it on any major userscript manager (like **Violentmonkey**, **Tampermonkey**, or **Greasemonkey**) via these official sources:

## ⚡ Key Interventions

### 1. Bionic Reading Engine (The Bold Feature)
* **What it is:** Automatically bolds the initial structural fixation points of every word.
* **The ADHD Hack:** Your eyes lock onto the bold anchors while your brain seamlessly completes the rest of the word. It stops your eyes from wandering, prevents sentence skipping, and forces text to feel highly "scannable."
* **Bulletproof Build:** Fully engineered to cross open Shadow DOM boundaries and dynamically inject into Single-Page Applications (SPAs) like Twitter, YouTube, or Reddit without layout breaking.

### 2. Reading Focus Lane
* **What it is:** Creates an adjustable cinematic ambient dimming mask over the page, exposing only a clean horizontal slit (the "Focus Lane") where you are reading.
* **The ADHD Hack:** It physically deletes peripheral visual clutter, moving advertisements, and sidebar distractions from your field of vision.

### 3. Continuous Auto-Scroll Engine
* **What it is:** A silky-smooth, hardware-accelerated scroller built via `requestAnimationFrame` to avoid layout thrashing.
* **Intelligent Resumption:** Moving your mouse cursor **will not** interrupt the scroll. The moment you actively interact via your mouse wheel, trackpad swipe, or keyboard navigation keys, the script instantly pauses to give your eyes time to process. It smoothly picks back up 1.2 seconds after you stop interacting.

### 4. Top Progress Bar
* A beautiful, non-intrusive gradient bar (`#30D158` to `#0A84FF`) across the top of your viewport showing your exact reading progress down to the percentage.

## 🎹 Global Key Bindings

* `Alt + S` : Toggle Auto-Scroll Play / Pause.
* `Mouse Wheel / Arrow Keys / Spacebar` : Temporarily pauses execution.
* UI Panel is entirely self-contained—featuring an Apple SF-Symbols-inspired sleek minimalist control pill at the bottom right.
